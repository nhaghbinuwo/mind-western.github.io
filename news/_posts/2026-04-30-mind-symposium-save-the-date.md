Mark your calendars!

We are delighted to announce the **MIND Platform Symposium: Mesoscopic Integrated Neuroimaging Data**, taking place on **September 22, 2026**, at Western University, London, Ontario.

This full-day event will bring together experts in MRI and lightsheet microscopy to explore cutting-edge advances in mesoscopic imaging, integrative data platforms, and translational brain research. The symposium will feature keynote presentations, rapid research highlights, a poster session, and networking opportunities to foster collaboration across scales and modalities.

Further details—including the full program, registration, and abstract submission guidelines—will be shared soon.

We look forward to welcoming you in September 2026!
